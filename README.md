# Top Grant Management Software Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Grant Lifecycle Management, Application Review & Funding Distribution*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Grant Management Software**. These tools help foundations, government agencies, and nonprofits manage the entire grant lifecycle — from accepting applications and coordinating reviews to distributing funds and tracking outcomes — while ensuring compliance and transparent reporting.

**Examples** include Salesforce Grantmaking, Submittable, Fluxx, Blackbaud Grantmaking, Foundant Technologies, SurveyMonkey Apply, WizeHive Zengine, SmartSimple, Good Grants, and AmpliFund (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom grant workflows, and transparent funding data — ideal for foundations seeking data sovereignty and developers building grant management platforms.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Grantmaking](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  
  Enterprise grant management within Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, extending the platform with grant and budget management objects. Manages funding opportunities, application review, budget tracking, and grantee reporting. Pricing starts at $175 per user/month for the Organization edition .

- **[Submittable](https://www.submittable.com/)**  
  Submission management platform used by organizations to build customized application forms, review submissions, and communicate with applicants. In 2025, managed $4.3B in funds across 1.18M applicants and 4.4M reviews. Handles grants, scholarships, fellowships, and creative work .

- **[Fluxx](https://www.fluxx.io/)**  
  Grants management platform for the full funding lifecycle, trusted by 10 of the 20 largest global foundations. Manages $39B+ in annual funding across 200,000+ grantees. Grantmaker starts at $13,000/year; Grantseeker at $6,000/year .

- **[Blackbaud Grantmaking](https://www.blackbaud.com/)**  
  Cloud-based solution for the full grant lifecycle, from accepting online applications to assessing and communicating outcomes. All-in-one CRM and grant management with reviewer portal, payment scheduling, and compliance tools. Includes branch functionality for managing multiple grantmaking entities with data separation .

- **[Foundant Technologies](https://www.foundant.com/)**  
  Grant management and foundation management solutions for social good organizations. Introduced Fia™ (Funder Intelligence Advisor) in 2026, an AI platform unifying data across grants, CRM, finance systems, and document storage with governance controls and citation trails .

- **[SurveyMonkey Apply](https://www.surveymonkey.com/)**  
  Application management platform built on SurveyMonkey infrastructure, handling grants, scholarships, and awards with review workflows and reporting .

- **[WizeHive Zengine](https://www.wizehive.com/)**  
  Highly configurable and open-architected platform for grants, scholarships, fellowships, and awards. Serves foundations, universities, associations, government offices, and healthcare institutions .

- **[SmartSimple](https://www.smartsimple.com/)**  
  Cloud-based automation and collaboration software for grant management and complex business processes. Serves corporate, academic, and government sectors with customizable solutions .

- **[Good Grants](https://goodgrants.com/)**  
  Grant management platform supporting the entire lifecycle with multi-currency and multilingual capabilities. Features application form builder, review workflows with scoring rubrics, automated notifications, and data residency options .

- **[AmpliFund](https://www.amplifund.com/)**  
  Grant management software for government and public sector organizations. Acquired by Euna Solutions in 2025, expanding public sector SaaS offerings .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Gandhi](https://github.com/mike-marcacci/gandhi)**  
  Open-source, online grant management system. AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Note**: The project shows limited recent activity (0 commits and issue activity in the last 90 days as of August 2026), with OpenSSF scorecard of 2.4/10 indicating potential maintenance concerns. Review carefully before production use .

- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/civicrm/civicrm-core)**  
  Leading open-source CRM for nonprofits with grant tracking capabilities. Features online and offline fundraising, grant tracking, event planning, donor management, and comprehensive reporting with 40+ templates. Integrates with WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla. Localized in 20+ languages .

- **[OpenEMIS Core](https://github.com/OpenEMIS/core)**  
  Open-source Education Management Information System with grant and funding management modules for educational institutions. PHP/JavaScript/MySQL stack, GPL licensed.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **ERPNext** — Open-source ERP with grant management and nonprofit modules adaptable for funding workflows.
- **Odoo** — Modular open-source ERP with CRM and project modules that can be configured for grant tracking.
- **CiviCRM Extensions** — Community-built extensions for grant-specific workflows and funder reporting.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **CiviCRM** for constituent and grant tracking, **ERPNext** for financial and project management, and **Metabase + PostgreSQL** for grant analytics dashboards to build a complete self-hosted grant management stack.
